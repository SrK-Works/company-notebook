## Exceptional item (note 40.1, PDF page 285, printed page 283)

"Impact of New Labour Codes 743": the Labour Codes, notified 21 November 2025, raised the provision for gratuity (retirement pay) and compensated absences (paid leave) by ₹743 million for continuing operations; presented as an exceptional item because it is material, "regulatory-driven" and non-recurring.

## Discontinued operations (note 58, PDF pages 328–329, printed pages 326–327)

58.1: the Energy business was demerged to Siemens Energy India Limited, effective 25 March 2025 with appointed date 1 March 2025 (court-approved scheme of arrangement); Energy results up to the appointed date are shown as discontinued operations. Profit before tax from Energy 5,585; from LVM 717 (note 47). 58.2: Board approved on 8 December 2025 the sale of the Low Voltage Motors business to Innomotics India Private Limited for an enterprise value of ₹22,000 million; approval received from the competition regulator; "yet to be consummated" at the report date; LVM is shown as discontinued and held for sale.
