## What the segments contain (BRSR, PDF page 148, printed page 146)

"Telecom Products - Manufacturing of optical fiber, optical fiber cables and telecom and networking products" (56.61% of the entity's turnover); "Turnkey Contract and Services — Engineering, Procurement and Construction Services" (43.37%). These percentages are for the standalone entity and are not used in the snapshot.

## What is not claimed

- Segment results are before interest and tax and before unallocated items; they are not net profit by business.
- No profit is assigned to individual products (fibre, cable, Wi-Fi, radios) or to any customer.
- Quarterly figures after March 2026 are not part of this snapshot.
