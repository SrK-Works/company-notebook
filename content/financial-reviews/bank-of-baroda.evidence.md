## Notes
- Consolidated note 17: "as a matter of prudence, the Bank decided to update one of the assumptions for actuarial calculation by shifting mortality rate table from IALM (2012-2014) ULT to IALM (2012-2015) ULT. Due to this change, an amount of Rs 52037 lakhs is provided during the quarter ended 31 March 2026." The standalone results carry the same note.
- Standalone results note (scan lists it between notes 17 and 19): "the Bank has received favourable orders from ITAT / Appellate authorities on tax matters pertaining to earlier assessment years ... during the quarter ended March 31, 2026, the Bank has reversed tax provision of Rs 129383 lakhs to its profit and loss account." The number of the equivalent consolidated note (note 16, referred to from the tax line) is not legible in the scan, so a group-level figure is not verified.
- Labour Codes note in both the standalone and consolidated results (note numbers not legible in the scan): impact on employee benefits "is not material".
- Segment note 18/19: segment revenue represents revenue from external customers; Digital Banking is a sub-segment of Retail.
