## Notes (PDF pages 13–14)

- Note 9: KMCC divested 30.99% of Infina Finance Private Limited on 24 March 2026 for ₹1,293.91 crore; "a pre tax gain of ₹367.79 crore (net of expenses) is recognised ... disclosed as an exceptional item". FY25 exceptional item ₹3,803.40 crore was the sale of 70% of Kotak Mahindra General Insurance to Zurich.
- Note 6: New Labour Codes provision for the year ended 31 March 2026 is ₹125.75 crore, inside employee cost (not an exceptional item).
- Note 13: Kotak Mahindra Investments Limited's lending moves into the bank from 1 April 2026.

## What is not claimed

- No profit is assigned to any product (cards, home loans, 811 accounts) or brand.
- Standalone bank figures are not mixed with these consolidated figures.
- Quarterly figures after March 2026 are not part of this snapshot.
