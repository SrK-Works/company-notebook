Unusual costs in FY 2025–26 were ₹1,024.68 million from the business combination and ₹386.23 million from a vendor settlement, totalling ₹1,410.91 million (₹141.09 crore rounded). The filing also reports ₹698.26 million of tax tied to the combination (₹69.83 crore rounded) and ₹786.39 million of other current tax. Note 3 attributes the incremental combination tax to exchange-rate movements and finalising the tax obligations. The prior-year annual column shows higher exceptional expenses of ₹13,464.34 million and tax on account of the combination of ₹24,868.42 million. Note 5 breaks out prior-year share-plan and employee tax costs as part of exceptional items.

Lesson check: Marketplace includes seller-buyer activity, paid ads, delivery and shopping via creators. New Initiatives includes local delivery for daily essentials, digital financial services and artificial-intelligence services. These activities come from the financial filing’s segment note. The [prospectus](https://investor.meesho.com/investor-web/_next/docs/ipo/meesho_limited_prospectus.pdf) explains the seller relationship, shipping fee revenue and Valmo partner roles. Meesho’s [January 2026 letter](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MEESHO_30012026174750_MLShareholdersletter30Jan2026.pdf) describes no commission and revenue led by shipping and ads. The [October 2026 shareholder letter](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MEESHO_28102026171517_MLshareholdersletter28102026.pdf) confirms the current commission-free Meesho Mall and explains the festive shift and current delivery cost context.
