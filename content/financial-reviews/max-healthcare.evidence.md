## PDF page 296 — notes 34.21 and 34.22
34.21: "…the incremental impact has been presented as 'Impact of new Labour Codes' under Exceptional Items… consisting of gratuity of ₹2,125 lakhs, long-term compensated absences of ₹1,109 lakhs, and other employee benefits of ₹156 lakhs primarily arises due to change in wage definition." (2,125 + 1,109 + 156 = 3,390.)
34.22: merger of Crosslay Remedies Limited with Jaypee Healthcare Limited, effective 15 December 2025 from appointed date 5 October 2024, accounted as a business combination of entities under common control, "resulting in cumulative tax benefit of ₹14,148 lakhs during the year ended March 31, 2026". "Provision of ₹1,434 lakhs has been made for the estimated stamp duty on the merger of JHL and CRL… disclosed as exceptional item."
