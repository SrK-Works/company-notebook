# Central Depository Services (India) Limited — financial verification

Checked 18 November 2026 against the [CDSL audited integrated annual report for 2025–26](https://www.cdslindia.com/downloads/IPO/GeneralMeeting/CDSL%20IR%202025-26.pdf) and the [signed audited September 2026 results](https://www.cdslindia.com/downloads/InvestorRels/CorporateGovernance/OutcomeBM31102026_Signed.pdf). Both use consolidated figures. The annual report prints amounts in ₹ lakh and the quarterly filing uses ₹ crore. One crore equals 100 lakh.

## Annual statement, PDF page 287

| Measure in ₹ lakh | FY26 | FY25 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Revenue from operations | 114,491.87 | 108,228.26 |
| Profit after tax for the whole group | 45,507.66 | 52,632.64 |

FY26 profit attributed to owners of CDSL was 45,613.18 lakh. The share attributed to other owners of subsidiaries was a loss of 105.52 lakh. The consolidated total after tax therefore comes to 45,507.66 lakh. The group performance discussion on PDF page 154 identifies growth in expenditure and lower investment income. The annual statement shows higher technology and employee benefit costs.

## Segment reporting, note 35, PDF page 339

| Reported segment | Revenue in ₹ lakh | Segment result in ₹ lakh |
|---|---:|---:|
| Depository Activity | 96,045.23 | 46,728.35 |
| Data Entry and Storage | 18,280.99 | 6,022.43 |
| Repository | 340.36 | −799.54 |
| Reported total | 114,666.58 | 51,951.24 |

Segment revenue includes 174.71 lakh billed between group companies. Subtracting this gives 114,491.87 lakh in consolidated operating revenue. Adding 8,947.31 lakh of unallocated income to the combined segment result gives 60,898.55 lakh in profit before tax. Subtracting 15,390.89 lakh of tax gives group profit after tax of 45,507.66 lakh. The segment result is the company’s reported segment measure before the group reconciliation. The report supplies no individual profit line for an account, transfer, or voting service.

## September quarter, PDF pages 10–11

The 31 October 2026 filing compares the quarters ended 30 September 2026 and 30 September 2025. Consolidated revenue from operations on PDF page 10, row 1(a), was ₹394.48 crore and ₹318.88 crore respectively. Total profit after tax on row 7 was ₹173.30 crore and ₹139.93 crore. The portion attributable to CDSL shareholders was ₹173.85 crore and ₹140.21 crore. The segment table on PDF page 11 shows the depository was the main contributor to the increase in operating revenue.
