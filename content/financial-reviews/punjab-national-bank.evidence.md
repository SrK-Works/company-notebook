The bank-only management segment table at PDF p. 13 uses a separate segment-results measure and a bridge for unallocated expenses and tax. It does not provide consolidated PAT attributable to owners by individual product. The next section uses the consolidated table from the annual report and reconciles the results to these audited group totals.

The same table gives Unallocated Expenses 7,151.37; Operating Profit (after deducting provisions other than tax) 27,081.66; Provision for Tax 9,985.89; Extraordinary items —; Share of Earning in Associates (Net) 1,370.93; Minority Interest 74.01; and Net profit 18,392.69. Reconciliation: 34,233.03 − 7,151.37 = 27,081.66; 27,081.66 − 9,985.89 + 1,370.93 = 18,466.70 group profit before outside shareholder attribution; 18,466.70 − 74.01 = 18,392.69 attributable to owners. Total income and final net profit agree with the audited NSE result above. The annual report uses assigned and shared costs, not individual product profits.
