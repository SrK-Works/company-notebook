## Reconciliation
- 79,387 − 2,072 = 77,315 (₹ crore, as printed); 77,315 + 21,653 + 347 = 99,315.
- 1,193.11 + 1,477.63 + 0.16 = 2,670.90 (matches 267,090 lakh); 2,670.90 − 715.86 = 1,955.04; − 45.05 = 1,909.99.
- 7,938,707 − 207,185 = 7,731,522 lakh.
No product-level profit was inferred.
