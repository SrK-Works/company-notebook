Fast moving electrical goods (FMEG): Fans, LED lighting and luminaires, switches, switchgears, solar products, pumps, conduits and domestic appliances. ## Labour Codes (note 32, PDF page 377, printed 374)

The Group has determined that these changes result in an increase in gratuity and leave obligation of H 200.64 Mn. The Group has presented increase in obligation as an expense under the head 'Employee Benefit Expense' in the statement of profit and loss for the year ended 31 March 2026." 200.64 ÷ 10 = 20.06 crore.
