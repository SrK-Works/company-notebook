## Discrepancies and unsupported items
No amount, label, period or scope in the snapshot disagrees with the filing: revenue from operations 19,151.59, profit attributable to owners 2,533.49, group profit 2,537.01, non-controlling interest 3.52, sale of goods 18,858.21, other operating revenues 293.38, geography split (India 18,043.67; outside India 1,107.92) and the joint venture figures (loss 59.93, 51%, share 30.57) all match. Points to note:
1. Page locators. Snapshot "PDF page 132 · printed page 261" ✔ (spread printed 260–261; the statement of profit and loss is the right-hand page). "Note 43 · printed page 309" ✔ (PDF page 156, spread 308–309). "Notes 7 and 45 · printed pages 284 and 313" ✔ (PDF pages 144 and 158). "Notes 29 and 53 · printed pages 301 and 332" ✔ (PDF pages 152 and 168; spread 332–333). "BRSR items 13 and 17 · printed pages 107–108": item 13 is on PDF page 55 (spread 106–107) and item 17 is on PDF page 56, spread 108–109; the snapshot's "107–108" is off by one for item 17. Minor locator error, values unaffected.
2. Profit definition. The snapshot row shows profit attributable to owners (2,533.49), labelled so ✔; group profit after tax 2,537.01 is in the notes ✔.
3. Share of JV loss. 59.93 × 51% = 30.56; the filing prints 30.57 (calculated on unrounded figures). The consolidated statement shows the share of loss in associates and joint venture as (30.09) in total: JV (30.57) plus associate profit 0.48 ✔.
4. One-off item not in the snapshot notes. Consolidated note 50 (PDF page 165): "the Group has recognised (as past service cost) ₹48.56 [crore] towards increase in liability of gratuity and compensated absences" because of the four New Labour Codes (in force 21 November 2025); it is not shown as an exceptional item for the group, so it sits inside ordinary costs. No exceptional item in FY 2025-26 (prior year voluntary retirement cost ₹24.79 crore).
5. Product mix. The "~97% bakery products and salted snacks" and "~3% dairy products" in the snapshot come from the business responsibility report (BRSR), not the audited statements, and are rounded ("~"); the filing's own wording is "% of total Turnover contributed".
