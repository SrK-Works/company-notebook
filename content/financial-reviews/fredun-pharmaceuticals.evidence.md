## Segment

Directors' report: "The Company operates only in a single segment i.e. Pharmaceutical Segment." No profit is disclosed by product line (generics, cosmetics, nutrition products, animal health) or by customer type.

## Consolidated statement, for comparison only (PDF pages 124–125)

Revenue from operations 63,332.65; other operating revenue 214.24; other income 365.60; total income 63,912.49; profit before tax 4,699.60; net profit for the year 3,262.09 (₹32.6209 crore). The same statement prints "Owners of the Company 3,279.00" and "Non controlling Interests -0.01", which add to 3,278.99, not to the net profit of 3,262.09 (they are close to total comprehensive income instead). Because of this internal inconsistency, the snapshot uses the standalone statement, where every line reconciles. The directors' report describes the subsidiaries as the wholly owned Fredun Retail Private Limited, which acquired One Pet Stop Private Limited in March 2025 and incorporated Wagr Retail Private Limited in December 2025. Consolidated revenue from operations equals the standalone figure, so the subsidiaries added no external sales in FY26.

## What is not claimed

- Per-share figures are not used; they would need adjusting for the July 2026 bonus issue.
- The Q1 FY27 press release (6 August 2026) is unaudited and not part of this snapshot.
