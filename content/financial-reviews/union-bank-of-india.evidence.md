# Union Bank of India — audited FY 2025–26 consolidated financial review

Checked 15 November 2026.

Primary source: [Union Bank of India, April 23, 2026 audited results](https://www.unionbankofindia.bank.in/pdf/financial-result-april-2026.pdf). PDF pages 4 and 6 visually checked. Use the consolidated, audited **year ended 31 March 2026** column, fourth numerical column on each page (not the March quarter or standalone). The filing reports ₹ lakh; divide by 100 for ₹ crore.

The four business segments plus the unallocated row reproduce the two disclosed totals. Retail's digital and non-digital subrows are excluded from the snapshot rows to avoid double counting. The filing explains in notes 1–3 that management makes allocations for income and costs that cannot be directly assigned, and that Digital Banking is a Retail Banking subsegment. Keep the unallocated line distinct from operating segments.

## Independent issuer cross-check — Integrated Annual Report

[Integrated Annual Report 2025–26](https://www.unionbankofindia.bank.in/pdf/Union-Bank-of-India-Integrated-Annual-Report-2025-26.pdf), consolidated Schedule 18, printed p. 336 (PDF page 688). The segment table shown there in crore confirms every current-year row and both totals. The same page shows ₹1,622 crore of income on income-tax refunds under Schedule 14, Other Income, as a disclosed component of FY26 group income. That is other income, not a reduction of the FY26 tax expense in the profit reconciliation.
