## Notes (PDF page 19)

- Note 3, year: "fair value gains of Rs 56 Crores on remeasurement of contingent consideration, Profit on sale of non-core assets of Rs 35 Crores, Asset write-down of Rs 69 Crores, impairment of Goodwill of Rs 16 Crores and Statutory impact of the New Labour Codes of Rs 26 Crores." (Rounded in the note: 56 + 35 − 69 − 16 − 26 = −20, matching (20.06).)
- Note 1 (quarter): margins in India improved on lower tea cost inflation, "partly offset by US tariff and coffee cost inflation in International Business".
- Tata Starbucks Private Limited is a joint venture (auditor's report, PDF page 17), so its café sales are outside revenue and only its share of profit or loss is in the (90.70) equity-method line, which is not split by entity here.

## What is not claimed

- No profit is assigned to individual brands (Tata Tea, Tata Salt, Tetley, Eight O'Clock, Tata Sampann, Ching's and others).
- The Tata Starbucks share of profit or loss is not separately stated in this filing and is not estimated.
- Quarterly figures after March 2026 are not part of this snapshot.
