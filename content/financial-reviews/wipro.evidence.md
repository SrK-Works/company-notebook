## Reconciliation
- Segment revenue 921,153 + 6,940 = 928,093. ✔ (92,115.3 + 694.0 = 92,809.3 crore)
- Segment results 158,646 + 559 = 159,205; less reconciling items 7,954 = 151,251 = results from operating activities. ✔ (crore: 15,864.6 + 55.9 = 15,920.5; − 795.4 = 15,125.1)
- IT Services segment result: 62,896 + 53,138 + 31,083 + 14,955 − 3,426 = 158,646. ✔
- 151,251 − 14,577 + 36,491 + 257 = 173,422 profit before tax. ✔ ; 173,422 − 40,767 = 132,655. ✔ ; 131,974 + 681 = 132,655. ✔
- Segment revenue 928,093 is 1,853 higher than income-statement revenues 926,240. The filing does not itemise the revenue reconciling item; the income statement separately shows foreign exchange gains of 1,853.
- 131,974 + 2,756 − 475 = 134,255. ✔ 2,756 − 475 = 2,281 (₹228.1 crore after tax).
- ₹ crore (÷10): IT Services revenue 92,115.3, result 15,864.6; IT Products revenue 694.0, result 55.9; total segment revenue 92,809.3; segment results before reconciling items 15,920.5; reconciling items −795.4; results from operating activities 15,125.1; unallocated within IT Services 342.6; finance expenses 1,457.7; finance and other income 3,649.1; associate and joint venture share 25.7; profit before tax 17,342.2; tax 4,076.7; profit for the year 13,265.5; equity holders 13,197.4; non-controlling 68.1; income-statement revenues 92,624.0; gap to segment revenue 185.3; Labour Code charge 275.6, tax on it 47.5, after tax 228.1; adjusted net income 13,425.5.
