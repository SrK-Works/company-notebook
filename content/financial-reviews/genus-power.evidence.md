## Revenue from operations, note 25 (PDF page 272, printed page 265)

Revenue from sale of goods 4,172.02; revenue from rendering of services 458.27; revenue from service concession arrangement (note 58) 70.15; other operating revenue: finance income from service concession arrangement (note 58) 29.20; scrap sales 7.70; government incentives 13.58; total 4,750.92. Check: sum = 4,750.92 ✔. Service-concession amounts used in the notes: 70.15 + 29.20 = 99.35.

Note 58 (PDF page 304, printed page 297): an Advanced Metering Infrastructure project for a state electricity board under a Design-Build-Finance-Own-Operate-Transfer model; "supply, installation and maintenance of 10,21,500 smart prepaid meters"; payment terms include a 10% mobilisation advance, 22.5% of meter cost monthly during installation, 7.5% after six months, and "AMISP service charges (monthly rentals)".
