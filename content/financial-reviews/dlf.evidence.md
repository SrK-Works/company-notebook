## Note 55, exceptional items (consolidated; PDF page 236)

₹ lakh: Recovery of amount impaired in earlier years 23,518.88 (₹235.19 crore); impact of past service cost on gratuity and compensated absences due to change in labour laws (3,227.29) (₹32.27 crore); total 20,291.59 (₹202.92 crore). Check: 235.19 − 32.27 = 202.92 ✔. Text of the note: a joint venture company, Twenty Five Downtown Realty Limited (formerly Joyous Housing Limited), defaulted on a loan from a housing finance company; the Group had made provisions against its investment and loan; a consent award now has the venture paying the Group ₹80,100.00 lakh (₹801.00 crore) with interest, of which ₹25,110.00 lakh (₹251.10 crore) is already received and the rest is secured by a mortgage over residential units. The Group reversed the earlier impairment of ₹23,518.88 lakh as exceptional income and treats ₹41,171.58 lakh (₹411.72 crore) as interest income, majorly accrued and impaired over time in earlier years, shown in other income.

## Segment information (note 49, consolidated, PDF page 235)

"the operations of the Group fall under colonization and real estate business, which is considered to be the only reportable segment." No segment profit is disclosed.

## Scope

Consolidated: the Holding Company plus its subsidiaries; joint ventures and associates are included only through the line "Share of profit in associates and joint ventures (net)". The Q4 FY26 results presentation (https://dlf.in/qu-result/Q4FY26-Results-Presentation.pdf, page 9 note 1; financial update on page 26) states DCCDL and other JVs are "not consolidated line-by-line and only DLF's share of profit/loss is accounted". The presentation uses a management format (revenue from operations 8,552; PAT after JV profits and exceptional items 4,408, ₹ crore) that differs from the audited statements; it is not used for the table.

## Lesson check (The Westpark)

Annual report, director's report/MD&A (PDF page 76): "The Westpark in Mumbai, the Company's maiden launch in this geography". Results presentation (PDF page 12 note 2 and project table): "The Westpark, Mumbai; DLF owns 51% share in the JV". The lesson sentence "DLF's first launch in that city" is therefore supported by "maiden launch in this geography" (Mumbai); the wording was kept and no claim removed.
