## Notes 66–68 (PDF page 400)

Note 66: Labour Codes notified effective 21 November 2025; "no material liability is envisaged". Note 67: settlement dated 15 January 2025 with an LNG supplier, US$285 million paid to the Parent Company for litigation over non-supply of LNG cargoes in FY 2022-23; ₹2,440.03 crore recognised as exceptional income in 2024-25. Note 68: West Asia situation in March 2026 disrupted LNG supplies; force majeure declared by Petronet LNG under its Qatar Energy contract on 3 March 2026; lower natural gas sales and transmission volumes in March 2026 than February 2026.

## Scope (list of subsidiaries, joint ventures and associates, PDF page 115)

Subsidiaries include GAIL Gas Limited (100%), GAIL Mangalore Petrochemicals Limited (100%), Konkan LNG Limited (93.50%). Joint ventures include Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited (22.50%) and Central UP Gas Ltd (25.00%); associates include Mahanagar Gas Limited (32.50%) and Indraprastha Gas Limited (22.50%). Joint ventures and associates are accounted by the equity method (share of profit, ₹1,504.21 crore, in the statement above).
