Segment revenues sum to 7521.61 and 7521.61 − 189.75 = 7331.86. Segment results sum to 1367.53 and 1367.53 − 1550.12 = −182.59. After exceptional items, 781.17 − 194.55 = 586.62 continuing profit. Adding 27.07 discontinued profit gives 613.69 for the group, which includes both the 600.34 belonging to owners of the parent and 13.35 belonging to other owners.

Consolidated note 2(c), PDF p. 37: after the Supreme Court upheld the ruling setting aside the arbitration award, the group reversed 1147.30 owing to contractor SEPCO in the books, and a further 240.90 was reversed after the Delhi High Court disposed of the interest and cost matter in its favour. These are reductions in recorded amounts owed, not sales. Note 2(d) (PDF p. 38) also records a 414.34 adjustment reducing claims against electricity suppliers. Note 10 (PDF p. 45) explains the categories netted in exceptional items; note 8 (PDF pp. 44–45) identifies one-time labour-rule adjustment expense of 13.24. Separately, note 9 (PDF p. 45) says the group included 169.46 of an investment’s upward asset revaluation in its share of that investment’s profit. The reported net is 963.76, not the sum of only these highlighted components.
