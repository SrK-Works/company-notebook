### Lesson check
The official annual report and issue prospectus describe generation, renewable, transmission, distribution, infrastructure and transport-related financing. In FY2026 audited results, the auditors list REC among group entities and Note 7 identifies REC as a subsidiary. The November 2026 exchange filing describes no-objection letters and outstanding conditions, which are not evidence that the merger was completed. The financial notes distinguish the accounting credit and exceptional provision from amounts actually received or paid.
