## Note 62, exceptional items (PDF page 48)

Project cost expensed off 410.70. In a subsidiary of BPRL, the operator (Total E&P Mozambique Area 1 Limitada) declared force majeure on 22 April 2021 because of the security situation in the north of the Cabo Delgado province in Mozambique, subsequently resolved during FY 2025–26; certain incremental costs related to the suspension and force majeure, which are abnormal costs, were expensed.

## Scope (ownership table in the basis of preparation, PDF page 9)

Subsidiaries are BPRL and its subsidiaries and a trust. Joint venture companies include Central UP Gas Limited (25.00%), Maharashtra Natural Gas Limited (22.50%) and Sabarmati Gas Limited (49.94%). Associates include Indraprastha Gas Limited (22.50%) and Petronet LNG Limited (12.50%). These are accounted by the equity method (share of profit in the statement above).
