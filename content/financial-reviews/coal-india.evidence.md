## Other disclosures
- Segment reporting (consolidated notes, PDF page 224): "The Company's main business is Coal mining... there are no separate reportable segments."
- Report introduction: "the Company did not have any exceptional items during the reporting period."
- Note 13.3.6: Labour Codes — "no material impact is expected on the financial statements."
- Auditor/C&AG commentary on note 12.1: revenue includes ₹3,142.76 crore of "Singrauli Punarsthapan Charge" (₹300 per tonne from 1 May 2025, levied by NCL); High Court of Jabalpur stayed it on 29 April 2025 for the petitioners; NCL issued credit notes/refunds to certain consumers.
