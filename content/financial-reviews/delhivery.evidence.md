## One business segment (PDF page 41, MD&A)

Delhivery states its service lines "are reported as one combined segment — 'Logistics Services' as per Ind AS 108". Revenue by service line is disclosed; profit by service line is not. Revenue by service line, ₹ million: Express Parcel 66,870.46 (63.64%); Part Truckload 22,540.29 (21.45%); Supply Chain Services 7,499.11 (7.14%); Truck Load 6,263.88 (5.96%); Cross-Border 1,193.29 (1.14%); Others 716.04 (0.68%); total 105,083.07. Sum check: 66,870.46 + 22,540.29 + 7,499.11 + 6,263.88 + 1,193.29 + 716.04 = 105,083.07. ✔

## What is not claimed

- The profit above is group profit after tax. It is not profit for any service, and no service profit is inferred from revenue shares.
- Delhivery's own "excluding Ecom Express integration cost" profit (₹3,212.24 million) is its own non-statutory measure (MD&A, PDF page 42); it is cited only in the lesson text and labelled as Delhivery's measure.
- Quarter figures (quarter ended 30 September 2026) are unaudited and come from the earnings presentation; they are not in this snapshot.
