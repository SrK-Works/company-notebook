## Discrepancies and unsupported items
No amount, label, period or scope in the snapshot disagrees with the filing: revenue from operations ₹231,546.0 million (snapshot 23,154.6 crore), profit for the year ₹35,446.0 million (snapshot 3,544.6 crore), the four product-group sales (7,716.77; 7,274.01; 3,638.12; 4,442.56), their total 23,071.46 and other operating revenue 83.14 all match after dividing by 10. Points to note:
1. Sources. The two nestle.in PDFs cited in the snapshot returned an access-denied page to a plain download and "target unreachable" through TinyFish, so they were not read. Exchange copies of the same filings were used: (a) the 21 April 2026 outcome of the board meeting with the audited financial results (standalone and consolidated), filed with BSE, https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/bd089f96-1587-4a24-81ed-a73acfeabe10.pdf; (b) the Annual Report 2025-26, filed with BSE on 5 June 2026, https://bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/65cd4ca1-41a5-4526-b920-66b4133a4e5a.pdf. The page numbers below are in the exchange copies.
2. One-off items missing from the snapshot notes. Profit of ₹3,544.6 crore includes a net exceptional credit of ₹120.78 crore and a tax write-back of ₹109.72 crore, both from one cause (closure of old income tax cases) plus two one-off charges; see "Exceptional items" below. The snapshot definition says "after expenses, exceptional items and tax" but gives no amount.
3. Locator wording. Snapshot location "Standalone annual results · year ended 31 March 2026; cross-checked with official Key Financial Figures". The statement of profit and loss is on PDF page 61 of the annual report copy (printed page 85); the product table is note 24 (PDF page 83, printed page 107); the segment note is note 42 (PDF page 105, printed page 129). The "Key Financial Figures" page was not separately read.
4. The snapshot's "sale of products" total 23,071.46 also appears in the results press release as "Total Sales of INR 23,071.5 crore" (management wording, same number).
5. Garbled characters. In the results filing, the note 6 table prints "~414.4" as "H14.4" and "401.0" as "H0l.0". They are not used from that copy; the clean figures (414.4 and 401.0) were read in the annual report note 4 (PDF page 67).

## Note 4 exceptional items (PDF page 67, printed page 91) and note 36(a) tax
FY 2025-26 exceptional items, net credit of ₹1,207.8 million: (a) income tax litigation closed for certain earlier years: provisions of ₹3,120.4 million made in earlier years written back, of which ₹1,097.2 million is in tax expense and ₹2,023.2 million in exceptional items; (b) restructuring cost charge of ₹401.0 million for severance compensation; (c) New Labour Codes: "incremental financial impact of ₹414.4 million as past service cost on post-employment defined benefits … driven by a regulatory change and … non-recurring in nature, it is classified under exceptional items". 2,023.2 − 401.0 − 414.4 = 1,207.8 ✔.
