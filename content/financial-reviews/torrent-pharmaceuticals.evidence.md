## Exceptional items (note 40, PDF page 202, printed pages 400–401)

FY 2025–26: "₹70.02 crores of regulatory and statutory fees, along with other related costs, associated with JB Pharma's acquisition as mentioned in Note 42 and severance compensation of ₹18.72 crores incurred on account of restructuring of the JB Pharma's distribution network." 70.02 + 18.72 = 88.74; statement shows 88.75 (₹0.01 crore rounding). FY 2024–25 (24.21): settlement of a 2017 demand from the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority over alleged overcharging.

## Acquisition of J.B. Chemicals & Pharmaceuticals Limited (note 42, PDF pages 203–204, printed pages 402–405)

On 21 January 2026 the Parent Company "acquired a controlling stake representing 46.39% of the equity share capital of JBCPL from Tau Investment Holdings Pte. Ltd. for a cash consideration of ₹11,917 crores"; further shares from employees for ₹620 crores; aggregate shareholding 48.80% on a fully diluted basis; JBCPL "became subsidiary" and four wholly owned subsidiaries of JBCPL also became subsidiaries. Total cash consideration ₹12,537.27 crore; net cash outflow net of cash acquired ₹12,314.95 crore. "The Scheme of amalgamation of JB Pharma into the Parent Company has been filed with the National Company Law Tribunal ('NCLT'), Ahmedabad Bench. Pursuant to the Scheme being effective, the Parent Company will issue 51 (Fifty one) fully paid-up equity shares of the Parent Company having face value of ₹5 each for every 100 fully paid-up equity share of ₹1 each of the JBCPL." (Not yet effective in the report.) Impact (part F): "The consolidated revenue of JBCPL from January 21, 2026 to March 31, 2026 is ₹775.40 crores with Consolidated Profit before tax of ₹191.87 crores." Pro-forma if combined from 1 April 2025: consolidated revenue ₹17,352 crores, profit before tax ₹2,570 crores, "net of amortisation and depreciation on fair value of identified assets". Non-controlling interests hold 51.20% of JBCPL (note 45, PDF page 204, printed 404–405); the non-controlling interest in profit for the year is a loss of ₹25.19 crore.

## Lesson check

The earlier lesson note that figures include J.B. Chemicals only from 21 January 2026 is sourced above (note 42). The lesson's claim of the merger being pending is limited to what note 42 says (filed with NCLT; not effective in the report).
