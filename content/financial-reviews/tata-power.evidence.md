## PDF page 16 and XBRL — notes to the consolidated results
- Note 3: Labour Codes: "additional expense of INR 74 crore towards gratuity and leave encashment liabilities. Of this amount, INR 62 crore pertains to the regulated business and ... has been considered as a pass through in tariff."
- Note 4: impairment provision of INR 94 crore on the investment in Adjaristsqali Netherlands B.V., a joint venture held for sale, "as an exceptional item".
- Note 5: "the Holding Company has recognized a deferred tax asset amounting to INR 250 crore on previously unrecognised business losses".
- Note 6: Tata Power Delhi Distribution Limited: regulatory assets of INR 296 crore and tax expense of INR 75 crore for the quarter; "during the year ... regulatory assets of INR 751 crore and tax expense of INR 189 crore" after tariff true-up orders.
- Note 7: SIAC awards (liability award 26 September 2023; quantum and final awards 1 July 2025 and 27 August 2025) directing the company to pay damages of USD 490,320,000 with simple interest of 5.33%, and costs; the company has appealed on legal advice and "no provision has been recorded".
- Note 2: Mundra plant operations suspended from 3 July 2025 for overhaul; supplementary power purchase agreement signed with GUVNL on 23 March 2026 effective 1 April 2025.
