## Scope: subsidiaries, joint venture, associate (PDF page 282, printed pages 280–281)

Entities included in consolidation: Cummins Sales & Service Private Limited, Subsidiary 100% at 31 March 2025, nil at 31 March 2026 (sold; ceased to be a subsidiary effective 1 April 2025, note 50); Valvoline Cummins Private Limited, Joint Venture 50%; Cummins Generator Technologies India Private Limited, Associate 48.74%. The joint venture and associate are accounted for by the equity method: only Cummins India's share of their after-tax profit (266.28) appears in the statement; their sales and costs are not added to revenue. So the consolidated revenue of 12,143.19 is essentially Cummins India's own.

## Exceptional items (note 50, PDF page 348, printed pages 346–347)

Labour Codes: impact of ₹94.20 crore, "material, regulatory-driven, and non-recurring", presented as an exceptional item. Impact for the joint venture and associate, a net expense of ₹8.36 crore, is inside "Share of profit of joint venture and associate after tax". Sale of 100% of Cummins Sales & Service Private Limited: gain ₹12.59 crore, recorded in the consolidated statements.

## Segment information (note 44, PDF page 342, printed pages 340–341)

Two segments: "Engines" (Cummins India and other group companies) and "Lubes" (a joint venture, Valvoline Cummins Private Limited, equity method). The Lubes figures are "full numbers in the Statement of Profit and Loss of Valvoline Cummins Private Limited and are not Group's proportionate share"; the 112.61 in the adjustment column is the 50% share included in group profit. Year to 31 March 2025: Engines sales 10,219.24; Lubes 2,352.27; PAT Engines 1,899.55; Lubes 200.78; adjustment 100.39; total 1,999.94. Not used as segment rows because Lubes revenue is not part of consolidated revenue and the basis differs.
