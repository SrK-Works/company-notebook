# National Securities Depository Limited — financial verification

Checked 18 November 2026 against the [NSDL audited annual report for 2025–26, filed with BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/a61bc02b-657b-4b53-892b-eb4b45f08350.pdf) and the [signed unaudited September 2026 results filed with BSE](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachHis/ff398d7a-ad88-4629-b98b-77a78c6687ae.pdf). Both use consolidated figures in ₹ lakh. One crore equals 100 lakh.

## Annual statement, PDF page 220

The FY26 statement reports profit before tax of 50,554.67 lakh. Profit attributable to NSDL shareholders was 37,973.84 lakh. Note 21 on PDF page 256 separately lists revenues for depository custody and transactions, banking services and corporate actions related to new issues.

The note prints profit before tax of 50,554.65 lakh and profit after tax of 38,001.19 lakh. Each is 0.02 lakh below the corresponding line in the consolidated income statement. The snapshot uses the income statement total for group profit and the note’s own segment results.

## September quarter, PDF page 9

The 31 October 2026 filing compares the quarters ended 30 September 2026 and 30 September 2025. Consolidated revenue from operations on row 1(a) was 54,589.33 lakh and 40,004.17 lakh. Consolidated profit after tax on row 7 was 12,245.37 lakh and 11,038.85 lakh. These profits are for the whole group, before the split by ownership. Total expenses were 42,020.08 lakh and 28,467.83 lakh respectively.
