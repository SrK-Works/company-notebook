# Rolex Rings Limited · FY 2025–26 review · 18 November 2026

Audited standalone company figures for the full year ended 31 March 2026. The [company annual report](https://www.rolexrings.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/ROLEX-RINGS-AR-2025-26-1.pdf), PDF page 131, is the standalone profit and loss statement. All printed values below are INR million. Divide by 10 for INR crore (display rounded).

The revenue and profit used in the record are ₹1,143.495 crore and ₹141.098 crore. The notes show exceptional charges of ₹51.64 crore rounded. Note 32 on PDF page 166 lists INR 504.00 million for the final settlement above amounts previously provided, and INR 12.41 million for labour code changes. Total cash paid to lenders on 31 March was INR 1,010 million (₹101 crore). The balance carried to the settlement from earlier periods was INR 506 million. The statement reconciles INR 2,419.30 million less INR 516.41 million to INR 1,902.89 million, then less INR 491.91 million to INR 1,410.98 million.

PDF pages 173–174, note 37, identify one reported segment and three buyers exceeding the disclosure threshold, for INR 4,261.42 million in total. There is no separate bearing ring or automotive components profit in the annual segment note. Pages 18–20 describe products and operations. The signed [September results](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/ROLEXRINGS_05112026141849_Outcome_of_Board_Meeting.pdf) also reproduce the March 2026 annual totals in the audited comparison column.
