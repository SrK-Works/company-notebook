## PDF page 13 — notes
- Note 4: previous year, exceptional gain of ₹3,945.73 crore on sale of 13.51% of AWL Agri Business. "During the year ended 31st March, 2026, ACLLP has sold remaining 30.42% of the paid-up equity share capital of AWL. Consequently, the Group recognized ₹8,600.81 crores (post-tax gain on ₹7,116.68 crores) and ₹5,632.09 crores (post-tax gain of ₹4,661.11 crore) as exceptional gain for the year ended 31st March, 2026 and for the quarter ended 31st December, 2025 respectively and AWL ceases to be an associate."
- Note 5: Adani Cementation merged into Ambuja Cements (effective 1 August 2025); the Parent Company received 87,00,000 Ambuja equity shares "and recorded exceptional gain of ₹614.56 crores".
- PDF page 14, note 15: the four Labour Codes took effect 21 November 2025; "financial implications of the same has been recognised in the current year". No amount is stated in the results.

## Reconciliation
- Segment revenue 28,362.46 + 4,445.86 + 5,642.30 + 15,399.00 + 13,215.43 + 6,347.18 + 15,284.34 + 14,289.60 = 102,986.17; less 2,517.56 = 100,468.61 = revenue from operations.
- Segment results sum 17,069.15; plus unallocable income 2,474.63 = 19,543.78; less finance costs 6,019.12 = 13,524.66 = profit before tax.
- 100,468.61 + 2,474.63 = 102,943.24 total income; 102,943.24 − 98,633.95 = 4,309.29; + 9,215.37 = 13,524.66.
- Exceptional 8,600.81 + 614.56 = 9,215.37.
- 3,884.44 − 53.29 = 3,831.15; 13,524.66 − 3,831.15 = 9,693.51; + 257.18 = 9,950.69; 9,339.47 + 611.22 = 9,950.69.
- The filing does not say which segment holds the exceptional gains; none was inferred. No product-level profit was inferred.
