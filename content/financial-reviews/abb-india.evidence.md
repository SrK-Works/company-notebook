## Segments (note 42, PDF pages 327–329, printed pages 325–327)

Segment descriptions (PDF pages 327–328): Motion segment: "motors, generators and drives provide power, motion and control for a wide range of automation applications"; Robotics and Discrete Automation: "value-added solutions in robotics, machine and factory automation"; Electrification: "technology across the full electrical value chain from substation to the point of consumption"; Process Automation: "products, systems and services designed to optimize the productivity of industrial processes ... turnkey engineering, control systems, measurement products, life cycle services, outsourced maintenance"; Power Grids (Discontinued).
