Income: (1,263,711,993 + 267,120,221) ÷ 10,000 = ₹153,083.2214 crore, shown as ₹153,083.22 crore.
Profit: (197,115,942 − 56,847 − 18,330,346) ÷ 10,000 = ₹17,872.8749 crore, shown as ₹17,872.87 crore.
The earlier NSE filing displays ₹17,872.88 crore at its coarser presentation precision; the annual report has the exact underlying units for this snapshot. The disposal is a loss, as it is signed in the annual report, regardless of the exchange note’s “surplus” wording.
