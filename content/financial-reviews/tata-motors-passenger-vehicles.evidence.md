## Scope after the demerger
Note 50(I) (PDF page 436, printed 431): the Composite Scheme of Arrangement demerged the commercial vehicles business to Tata Motors Limited (formerly TML Commercial Vehicles) and amalgamated Tata Motors Passenger Vehicles Limited into the company; NCLT order 25 August 2025, appointed date 1 July 2025, effective 1 October 2025. The commercial vehicles business is shown as a discontinued operation, and FY 2024-25 is re-presented the same way. Segments from 1 July 2025 (note 44, PDF page 421): Automotive (Tata Passenger Vehicles and Jaguar Land Rover) and Others (IT services). All table figures below are for continuing operations unless stated.
