## What is not claimed

- Total income is gross income, not sales and not net interest income. No net interest income figure is stated in the snapshot.
- No profit is assigned to loan types (solar, wind, hydro and so on); the company does not disclose profit by loan type.
- Quarterly figures after March 2026 are not part of this snapshot.
