### Arithmetic
30,784.66 + 438.65 + 633.85 = 31,857.16. 31,857.16 − 300.76 + 26.22 = 31,582.62. 6,402.09 + 87.82 + 111.65 = 6,601.56. 6,601.56 − 44.04 + 4.86 = 6,562.38. 6,562.38 − 1,671.47 = 4,890.91. 4,846.10 + 44.81 = 4,890.91.

### Lesson check
Financing Activity includes household and corporate borrowing plus certain investments (PDF p. 466); Investment Activity covers other investments and Others includes advisory, distribution, wealth management and operating leases. Neither segment income nor result is assigned to an individual brand or loan type. Combined figures cover Tata Capital and controlled companies, including housing finance. The FY2026 table ends before the approved proposed Yogloans acquisition in July 2026 (company annual report subsequent-development discussion and [company release](https://www.tatacapital.com/media-center/press-releases.html)). No assumption of historical gold-loan earnings.
