### Lesson check
The company's published [gold-loan process](https://www.muthootfinance.com/gold-loan/process) explains the pledge, appraisal and release; its [auction policy](https://www.muthootfinance.com/auction-policy) explains recoveries and gold price exposure. The results packet’s investor presentation describes Muthoot Homefin, Belstar, Muthoot Money, and the insurance broker; the controlled companies are also listed in consolidated results note 1. The financial interpretation draws from the profit-and-loss statement above and does not assume posting an interest charge means receiving cash.
