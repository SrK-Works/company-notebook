# Zydus Lifesciences Limited — annual figures verification

Checked 15 November 2026. Source: [FY 2025–26 Integrated Annual Report](https://www.zyduslife.com/investor/admin/uploads/14/2/2025-2026.pdf). Cross-check: [Audited results filed with the National Stock Exchange on 19 May 2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/Cadilahc_19052026141020_OutcomeofBM.pdf), PDF pages 18–20. Use the audited year ended 31 March 2026 column; the March quarter is not the annual column. The annual report uses ₹ million, converted to displayed ₹ crore by dividing by 10. Consolidated scope covers the parent and subsidiaries; joint ventures enter through their share of profit. The following is a numerical transcription of the figures used, with labels retained where relevant.

**Segment information:** annual PDF page 363, printed page 455, Note 44(A), same paired figures as the exchange-filed table on PDF page 19. The issuer shows no separate intersegment adjustment between these rows and group revenue from operations.

| Segment | Revenue (₹ million) | Result (₹ million) | Revenue (₹ crore) | Result (₹ crore) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Pharmaceuticals | 224,121 | 70,488 | 22,412.1 | 7,048.8 |
| Consumer Products | 39,540 | 2,671 | 3,954.0 | 267.1 |
| Medical technologies | 7,823 | −1,782 | 782.3 | −178.2 |
| Total revenue from operations / profit before exceptional items and tax | 271,484 | 71,377 | 27,148.4 | 7,137.7 |

The row sums reconcile exactly. The profit result is after finance costs included in consolidated expenses. This is not net profit, EBITDA, brand profit or cash flow. Annual PDF page 364, printed page 456, Note 44(C) reports ₹33,642 million from a single outside customer contributing at least 10% of group revenue; 33,642 ÷ 271,484 ≈ 12.4%. Note 44 identifies Medical technologies as a segment added following Amplitude’s acquisition. Annual PDF page 364, printed page 456, Note 45 lists Zydus Wellness as a subsidiary. The staged lesson distinguishes the subsidiary from the separately listed parent.

**Bridge to profit:** annual PDF page 307, printed page 399, consolidated profit and loss, checked against exchange PDF page 18. ₹71,377 million before exceptional expense and tax less ₹5,166 million exceptional expense gives ₹66,211 million before tax and share of joint-venture profit; tax ₹15,947 million reduces it to ₹50,264 million. Add ₹971 million of joint-venture profit after its tax to reach group profit ₹51,235 million. Deduct ₹835 million belonging to other owners to arrive at parent-owner profit ₹50,400 million. Display units respectively: 7,137.7; 516.6; 6,621.1; 1,594.7; 5,026.4; 97.1; 5,123.5; 83.5; 5,040.0 crore. The same page records net foreign-currency gain of ₹12,805 million (₹1,280.5 crore), in expenses as a negative, not in revenue.

**Unusual costs and tax:** annual PDF page 377, printed page 469, Note 48; exchange filing PDF pages 19–20 Notes 5–12. The ₹5,166 million total comprises ₹3,975 million settlement paid to end patent litigation, ₹849 million estimated increase in retirement and unused-leave benefits owed under the new Labour Codes, ₹245 million costs of buying Comfort Click and ₹97 million costs of winding up Naturell while transferring its running business to Zydus Wellness. Converted: ₹397.5, ₹84.9, ₹24.5 and ₹9.7 crore, summing to ₹516.6 crore. The ₹6,625 million prepaid licence fee in exchange Note 7 was recorded as an asset and is not part of the settlement expense or the ₹5,166 million charge. Tax Note 42 (annual PDF page 362, printed page 454) records reductions of ₹515 million and ₹826 million from recalculating deferred tax at changed rates for the parent and Indian subsidiaries, respectively. Their sum is ₹1,341 million (₹134.1 crore) of reductions in the tax reconciliation, not a cash receipt or a reduction in the total annual tax bill versus the prior year. The ₹15,947 million annual tax expense is the actual reported figure after all adjustments. The new tax basis also affects comparability.

**Lesson check:** the finished medicines, ingredients, consumer product and medical-device groups are supported by the issuer’s operations and products pages, the [FY26 presentation](https://zyduslife.com/investor/admin/uploads/15/0/May-2026.pdf), and annual Note 44. The [October 2025 exchange notice](https://www.zyduslife.com/investor/admin/uploads/21/82/Update-on-Acquisition-of-Shares-of-Amplitude-26-Oct-2025.pdf) confirms both the initial July purchase and the October purchase of the remaining shares. No product profit was inferred.
