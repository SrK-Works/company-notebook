# KFin Technologies Limited · FY 2025–26 verification

Checked 18 November 2026. [Signed audited annual filing on the company’s investor site](https://investor.kfintech.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/Intimation-42-1.pdf), PDF pages 48 and 49, audited consolidated annual column ending 31 March 2026. Page 48 is the profit and loss statement. Page 49 reports segment revenue and results and reconciles the latter to accounting profit. The source prints millions of rupees, divided by ten for crore.

The segment revenue rows add exactly to the reported ₹13,014.93 million consolidated revenue from operations. Segment result is earnings before interest and tax, central administrative costs and other income. Adding the allocated rows reproduces ₹5,988.24 million. The unallocated and finance lines net to ₹4,803.91 million with other income. After the exceptional and tax lines, group profit is ₹3,437.12 million (₹343.712 crore, shown as ₹343.71 crore). The employee benefits charge is ₹12.594 crore (shown as ₹12.59 crore). These amounts exclude standalone-only results, media releases and management's adjusted calculations.

Its September 2026 and September 2025 columns show revenue of ₹4,017.96 and ₹3,092.34 million and group profit of ₹1,003.42 and ₹933.13 million, respectively. International and other investor segment revenue was ₹1,109.84 and ₹430.50 million, and segment results were ₹48.54 and ₹100.91 million, respectively.
