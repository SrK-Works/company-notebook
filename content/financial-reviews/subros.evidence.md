Deducting ₹54.51 crore in tax gives ₹165.65 crore after tax. The charge reflects a reassessment of employee benefit obligations. Note 5 records a Gujarat government grant of ₹1,478 lakh (₹14.78 crore) under other income. Share of joint venture profit is included using the equity method, meaning the accounts include Subros’s share of its result. Note 4 identifies Thermal products as the only reported segment.
